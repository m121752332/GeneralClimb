霍將軍爬101大樓 · 遊戲說明

紅衣攀爬者「霍將軍」從台北 101 的 1F 徒手爬上塔尖，一共 14 關。遇到強風、下雨、老鷹就停下來抓牢，一旦墜落就從 1F 重來。這份說明照遊戲流程整理，可以用目錄（手機點左上 ☰）或上方搜尋找想看的段落。

General Huo Climbs 101 · Player guide

The climber in red, General Huo, climbs Taipei 101 bare-handed from 1F to the spire tip, across 14 levels. Stop and hold on when wind, rain or an eagle comes. Fall once and you start over from 1F. This guide follows the order you meet things in the game: use the contents (☰ on phones) or the search box at the top.

放開＝抓牢Let go = hold on不按方向鍵、手指離開，將軍就緊抓牆面，風險值會下降。With no key or finger down the climber grips the wall and risk goes down.
危機時不要動Freeze in a crisis強風、下雨、老鷹出現時，亂動就會被吹走、手滑或被撲下來。Moving in wind, rain or with an eagle out gets you blown off, slipping or knocked down.
墜落回 1FA fall means 1F不論在第幾關掉下去，都從 1F 重新開始，解鎖的關卡也會重新上鎖。Whatever level you fall from, you restart at 1F and the unlocked levels lock again.

啟動與載入

打開網頁會先看到載入畫面，依序完成六個項目才會進入主畫面：

  1. 讀取遊戲設定：關卡資料，完成時顯示「14 關」。
  2. 載入帳號：已登入 Google 會顯示名稱，沒登入就是「訪客模式」，旁邊附上你的最佳紀錄。
  3. 天氣系統：風、雨、老鷹。
  4. NPC 載入：窗戶裡的路人、員工、觀光客。
  5. 音效：顯示目前音量（靜音時會註明）。
  6. 場景渲染：主畫面與圖示。

任何一項失敗會顯示「載入失敗」與 重試 按鈕，按下去重新載入即可。雲端無法連線時不會卡住，會改用訪客模式繼續。

Starting up

The page opens on a loading screen. Six steps must finish before the main menu appears:

  1. Game settings: the level data (it reports "14 levels").
  2. Account: your Google name if signed in, otherwise "guest mode", with your best record.
  3. Weather: wind, rain and eagles.
  4. NPCs: the people behind the windows.
  5. Sound: your current volume (and whether it is muted).
  6. Rendering: the main menu and its icons.

If a step fails it says so and shows a Retry button. If the cloud cannot be reached the game carries on in guest mode.

開場動畫

每次打開網頁，主畫面上會先播一段約 3 秒的開場插畫：紅衣攀爬者的背影與台北天際線，慢慢放大後淡入主畫面。同一次開網頁只播一次。

按下「開始遊戲」或從選關進入時，還有約 2 秒的鏡頭拉近塔底。這兩段都可以點擊或按任意鍵略過（剛開始的一瞬間不接受，避免同一下點擊直接跳過）。墜落重來不會再播。

Opening

Each time you open the page, a 3-second opening plays over the menu: the climber in red from behind, the Taipei skyline, a slow zoom and a fade into the menu. It plays once per visit.

Starting a game (or a level from Levels) adds a 2-second camera push in to the foot of the tower. Both can be skipped by tapping or pressing any key (not in the first instant, so the click that started it does not skip it). Restarting after a fall does not replay them.

語言與全螢幕

切換語言

點右上膠囊的「🌐 中文 / EN」在繁體中文與英文之間切換，主畫面會重新載入一次。選擇會記在這台裝置上，下次打開維持同一種語言。

全螢幕

點膠囊右半邊的「全螢幕」。電腦與 Android 瀏覽器會直接進入全螢幕。

iPhone：Safari 不提供網頁全螢幕。請用 Safari 的「分享 → 加入主畫面」，之後從主畫面圖示打開就是全螢幕；從主畫面開啟時按全螢幕會提示「已是全螢幕」。

Language and fullscreen

Language

Tap "🌐 中文 / EN" on the top-right pill to switch between Traditional Chinese and English. The menu reloads once. Your choice is remembered on this device.

Fullscreen

Tap "Fullscreen" on the right half of the pill. Desktop and Android browsers go fullscreen straight away.

iPhone: Safari has no fullscreen for web pages. Use Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen and open the game from that icon. Opened that way, the button tells you it is already fullscreen.

Google 登入與帳號

不登入也能完整遊玩，進度存在這台裝置的瀏覽器。登入 Google 後，登頂次數與最快時間會上傳到雲端，登上排行榜。

登入

點右上「Google 登入」，會跳出 Google 的登入視窗。如果看到「瀏覽器擋住了登入視窗」，請允許這個網站的彈出視窗再按一次。

帳號面板

登入後點帳號膠囊打開帳號面板，可以：

  • 立即同步：手動上傳還沒同步的登頂，旁邊顯示上次同步的時間。
  • 玩家名稱 → 修改：改排行榜上顯示的名字（規則見下方）。
  • 登出：回到訪客模式，裝置上的進度保留。
  • 刪除帳號（危險區域）：永久刪除雲端的登頂次數與排行榜紀錄，會再請你用 Google 確認一次，無法復原。這台裝置上的進度會留下來當訪客進度。
  • 隱私權政策、服務條款連結。

玩家改名規則

  • 去掉頭尾空白後 2–12 個字。
  • 只能用中英文、數字、底線 _、空白、連字號 -。
  • 名字後面的 #編號 由系統產生，不能修改，用來分辨同名玩家。
  • 改名後要等 30 秒才能再改；太頻繁會提示稍後再試。
  • 如果你已經在排行榜上，榜上的名字會一起更新，次數與時間不變。

沒有改過名的話，名稱取自 Google 顯示名稱（最多 12 字）；角色頭上的名牌也會顯示這個名稱，訪客顯示「霍將軍」。

Google sign-in and account

You can play everything without signing in; progress is kept in this browser. Signing in with Google uploads your summits and fastest time so they appear on the ranking.

Signing in

Tap "Sign in" at the top right and a Google window opens. If the game says the browser blocked the sign-in window, allow pop-ups for this site and try again.

Account panel

When signed in, tap the account chip:

  • Sync now: upload summits that are not synced yet. The last sync time is shown next to it.
  • Player name → Edit: change the name on the ranking (rules below).
  • Sign out: back to guest mode; progress on this device stays.
  • Delete account (danger zone): permanently deletes your cloud summits and ranking entry after you confirm with Google again. Progress on this device stays as guest progress.
  • Links to the privacy policy and terms.

Name rules

  • 2–12 characters after trimming spaces at both ends.
  • Letters (Chinese or English), digits, underscore _, space and hyphen - only.
  • The #number after your name is made by the system and cannot be changed; it tells players with the same name apart.
  • Wait 30 seconds between changes.
  • If you are already on the ranking, your entry is renamed too; counts and times stay.

Until you change it, your name comes from your Google display name (up to 12 characters). The name tag above the climber shows it too; guests see "Gen. Huo".

Settings and sound

  • Volume: sound effects and music. The speaker button on the menu mutes in one tap.
  • Vibration: vibration feedback on phones that support it.
  • Flashes: turn off lightning and the crisis screen flash if you are sensitive to flashing light.

The game version (for example "Version v1.0.0") is shown under the Done button; include it when you report a problem.

Browsers only allow sound after your first tap or key press, so silence right after the page opens is normal. All music and sound effects are original and synthesised live.

攀爬規則

101 的外牆被切成一格一格的窗格，每層樓 12 列。你可以繞著塔的四面爬，鏡頭跟著繞塔軸旋轉。

安全欄與危險欄

每一面靠近轉角的欄是危險區，中間是安全區。待在危險區風險值會一直上升；繞到另一面一定會經過轉角，要快速通過。越往上牆越窄，安全欄越少。竹節（第 4–11 關）每節最上面兩層樓是外斜的「花瓣」，兩側各多一欄危險區。

障礙物

牆上灰色的出風口設備不能抓，要往旁邊繞開。每一關都保證有一條只走安全欄就能到頂的路。

平台

多數關卡的起點是一個平台（塔底廣場、竹節的外展平台、頂部屋頂）。站在平台上可以左右走動，沒有危險區、風險值會下降，只有天氣事件仍然算數。

尖塔（第 13–14 關）

尖塔改成爬梯：只能上下，不能左右。強風時一樣要抓牢。

窗戶裡的路人、員工、觀光客會對你揮手或拍照，看到你遇到危險或墜落時會露出擔心的表情。純屬氣氛，不影響遊戲。

How climbing works

The facade of 101 is a grid of window cells, 12 rows per floor. You can climb round all four faces; the camera turns round the tower with you.

Safe and danger columns

On each face, the columns next to the corners are danger zones and the middle ones are safe. Risk keeps rising while you are in a danger column. Going round to another face always crosses a corner, so do it quickly. The walls get narrower higher up, leaving fewer safe columns. On the pagoda sections (levels 4–11) the top two floors of each section flare outwards and add one more danger column on each side.

Obstacles

Grey vent units on the glass cannot be gripped; go round them. Every level always has a route to the top that uses only safe columns.

Platforms

Most levels start on a platform (the plaza, a section ledge, the roof). On a platform you can walk sideways, there is no danger zone and risk goes down. Weather events still count.

The spire (levels 13–14)

The spire is a ladder: up and down only. Hold on in strong wind just the same.

People behind the windows wave or take photos as you pass and look worried when you are in danger or fall. It is just for atmosphere.

危機事件：強風、下雨、老鷹

事件來之前約 1 秒會先出現警告，左上角有事件圖示（風／雨／鷹）與剩餘時間條。同一時間只會有一個事件：前一個結束後至少隔 4 秒才會來下一個。

🌬 強風

持續 10–20 秒。強風中移動會每秒被吹向較近的轉角一格（危險區），風險值也大漲。完全不動就沒事。

🌧 下雨

持續 20–30 秒，開始時有閃電。手會滑：雨中連續移動超過約 0.3 秒就會滑落。完全停下，等雨停再爬。

🦅 老鷹

出現約 3 秒。只要一動，老鷹就會紅眼盯上你，0.6 秒後俯衝撲向你被盯上時的位置。一動都不要動；被盯上後趕快橫向或縱向離開那個位置還有機會躲過。

事件第一次出現（遊戲時間）之後
強風約 1 分鐘每分鐘擲一次機率，機率依關卡從 0%（第 1 關）升到 70%
老鷹約 1 分 30 秒每 2 分鐘
下雨約 10 分鐘每 10 分鐘

Crisis events: wind, rain, eagle

About 1 second before an event there is a warning, and its icon (wind / rain / eagle) with a time bar shows at the top left. Only one event happens at a time, with at least 4 seconds between one ending and the next starting.

🌬 Strong wind

10–20 s. Moving in the wind pushes you one cell towards the nearer corner (a danger column) every second, and risk shoots up. Keep still and nothing happens.

🌧 Rain

20–30 s, starting with lightning. Your hands slip: moving for more than about 0.3 s in a row makes you fall. Stop completely until it is over.

🦅 Eagle

Out for about 3 s. Any move and its eyes turn red; 0.6 s later it dives at where you were when it locked on. Do not move at all. If it has locked on, get out of that spot fast and it may miss.

Unlocks and rounds

  • Clear one, open one: clearing level N opens level N+1 in Levels. It stays open after you close the page, so you can continue from there.
  • A fall resets everything: after a fall only level 1 is open again.
  • Reaching the top completes a round: after the summit ending your round count and summit count go up by one, the levels lock again and the next round begins.

The menu shows "Round N · X summits". This progress lives in this browser; clearing the site's data clears it. When signed in, summits are synced to the cloud.

Reaching the top

When you finish level 14 at the spire tip, a 10-second ending plays: General Huo sits on the edge of the summit with the cape streaming in the wind, while the camera moves in, rises, pulls back and circles over the streets of Taipei. "You reached the tip!" appears midway.

After 1.5 s you can tap or press Space to skip it. Then the summit result shows:

  • real climbing time and game time
  • the seed
  • "Round N done · X summits"; the levels lock again

If you are signed in, the summit is added to the cloud straight away. Tap or press Space to play again, or "Exit" to go back to the menu.

練習模式

主畫面「練習模式」是一棟三層樓平房，遠方看得到 101。適合第一次玩的人熟悉操作與三種事件。

  • 強風、下雨、老鷹各示範一次，依序出現在房子約 10%、40%、70% 的高度。
  • 懲罰比正式關卡溫和：強風推移比較慢、風險漲比較慢、雨天容許時間與老鷹盯上的時間都比較長；事件長度用最短的時間。
  • 天氣還沒示範完之前，最後一步爬不上屋頂，畫面會提示「抓牢等一下再上屋頂」。
  • 墜落就回地面再練一次；爬上屋頂顯示「練習完成」。
  • 不計任何紀錄：不影響最佳紀錄、解鎖、周目與排行榜。

想直接進練習，可以在網址後面加 ?practice=1。

Practice mode

"Practice" on the menu is a three-storey house with 101 in the distance, a good first stop to learn the controls and the three events.

  • Wind, rain and an eagle each come once, at about 10%, 40% and 70% of the house's height.
  • Penalties are gentler: slower wind push and risk, more time before rain makes you slip and before the eagle spots you; events use their shortest length.
  • Until every demo has run, the last step onto the roof is blocked and the game tells you to hold on and wait.
  • Fall and you go back to the ground to try again; reach the roof and practice is complete.
  • Nothing is recorded: best record, unlocks, rounds and the ranking are untouched.

To go straight to practice, add ?practice=1 to the game's address.

常見問題

打開網頁沒有聲音？

瀏覽器要等你第一次點擊或按鍵後才會出聲。如果點了還是沒聲音，看看主畫面右上的喇叭鈕是不是靜音，或到設定調整音量。

手機上要怎麼爬？

在畫面下半部按住拖曳就是搖桿，手指離開就是抓牢；上半部左右拖曳是轉視角。建議橫拿或加入主畫面全螢幕玩。

為什麼我沒按，角色卻往旁邊移？

那是強風把你往轉角吹。強風只在你移動時才會推你，放開方向鍵就不會被推。

老鷹明明走了，我還是被撲下來？

老鷹一旦紅眼盯上你就一定會俯衝，就算你停下來也一樣。被盯上時要立刻離開原位（上下或左右），距離夠遠才躲得過。最好的辦法是老鷹出現時完全不動。

從選關中途開始，墜落為什麼回到 1F？

這是遊戲的規則：不論從哪一關開始，墜落一律回 1F，解鎖的關卡也重新上鎖。

登頂了，排行榜次數卻沒增加？

先確認已經登入 Google。訪客的登頂會在登入後自動補上傳。另外為了防止灌次數，兩次計入雲端的登頂之間要隔一段時間，太快的登頂會先存在裝置上，之後同步時再補上，不會遺漏。可以在帳號面板按「立即同步」。

最快時間為什麼沒有紀錄？

最快時間只算從第 1 關一次爬到塔尖、中途沒有墜落的那一趟。從選關接著玩、練習模式都不計時。也要登入 Google 才會上榜。

換手機或換瀏覽器，進度還在嗎？

周目、解鎖與最佳紀錄存在原本裝置的瀏覽器。登入同一個 Google 帳號後，雲端的登頂次數與排行榜紀錄會跟著帳號走。

「瀏覽器擋住了登入視窗」怎麼辦？

允許這個網站的彈出視窗後再按一次「Google 登入」。在 App 內建的瀏覽器（例如通訊軟體裡開的連結）可能無法登入，請改用 Safari 或 Chrome 打開。

畫面閃爍讓我不舒服？

到設定關掉「閃光效果」，閃電與危機時的畫面閃爍都會停止。

怎麼刪除我的資料？

帳號面板 → 刪除帳號，會永久刪除雲端紀錄與登入帳號。裝置上的進度可用瀏覽器的「清除網站資料」移除。詳見隱私權政策。

FAQ

There is no sound when the page opens.

Browsers only play sound after your first tap or key press. If it is still silent, check the speaker button at the top right of the menu, or the volume in Settings.

How do I climb on a phone?

Drag in the lower half of the screen to use the stick; lift your finger to hold on. Drag sideways in the upper half to turn the view. Holding the phone sideways or adding the game to your home screen helps.

Why did I move sideways without pressing anything?

That is strong wind pushing you towards a corner. The wind only pushes you while you move, so let go of the keys.

The eagle had gone but I was still knocked off.

Once its eyes turn red the eagle always dives, even if you stop. When it locks on, leave that spot straight away (up, down or sideways); only far enough away will it miss. The best plan is not to move at all while it is out.

I started from a later level; why does a fall send me to 1F?

That is the rule: whichever level you start from, a fall always sends you back to 1F and the unlocked levels lock again.

I reached the top but my count on the ranking did not go up.

Make sure you are signed in; guest summits are uploaded after you sign in. To stop count farming, cloud summits must be some time apart. Summits made faster than that are kept on the device and added on a later sync, never lost. You can press "Sync now" in the account panel.

Why do I have no fastest time?

Only a run from level 1 to the tip in one go without falling is timed. Runs continued from Levels and practice are not. You also need to be signed in to appear on the ranking.

Does my progress follow me to a new phone or browser?

Rounds, unlocks and best record stay in the original browser. Your cloud summit count and ranking entry follow your Google account.

"The browser blocked the sign-in window"

Allow pop-ups for this site and tap "Sign in" again. In-app browsers (links opened inside chat apps) may not support sign-in; open the game in Safari or Chrome instead.

The flashing bothers me.

Turn off "Flashes" in Settings to stop lightning and the crisis flash.

How do I delete my data?

Account panel → Delete account permanently removes your cloud records and sign-in account. Progress on the device can be removed with the browser's "clear site data". See the privacy policy.
